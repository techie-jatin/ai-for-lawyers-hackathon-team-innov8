## 🛠️ Pre-Flight Recording & Technical Setup Checklist

### 1. Screen & Display Settings
- **Display Resolution**: 1920 × 1080 (1080p at 60 FPS recommended).
- **Browser Zoom Level**: **110%** (Command/Ctrl + `+` once). This ensures fine legal citations, statutory section numbers, and badge tooltips remain readable on mobile screens.
- **Browser Cleanliness**: Use Chrome or Brave in a dedicated clean window. **Hide the bookmarks bar** (`Ctrl+Shift+B`), close unrelated extensions, and mute all operating system notifications (`Focus Assist` / `Do Not Disturb` on).

### 2. Audio & Microphone Settings
- **Microphone**: USB condenser or headset mic positioned 3–5 inches from mouth with pop filter.
- **Voice Delivery**: Confident, deliberate, authoritative legal-tech cadence (130–145 words per minute). Avoid rushing through statutory citations.
- **Background Music**: Low-volume modern corporate tech / ambient electronic track at **8% volume**. Enable software ducking so background audio dips whenever speaking.

### 3. Browser Tabs Pre-Arranged (Left to Right)
- **Tab 1 (Primary)**: `https://ai-for-lawyers-hackathon-team-innov-tau.vercel.app/` (Fresh guest session, clean chat canvas).
- **Tab 2 (Contract Studio)**: Live App switched to *AI Contract Studio* tab.
- **Tab 3 (Team Profile)**: `https://hackindia.org/2026/ai-for-lawyers-hackathon/teams/team-innov8` (Showing connected Sharp wallet address).
- **Tab 4 (GitHub Repo)**: Clean GitHub repository with verified badges and architecture diagram.
