## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an institutional, authoritative, and uncompromisingly precise interface tailored to senior legal advocates, corporate counsels, judiciary researchers, and citizens navigating Indian jurisprudence. Blending the deliberate rigor of elite legal chambers with the hyper-focused, utilitarian ergonomics of modern engineering workspaces (evoking Harvey AI and Linear), the visual aesthetic rejects decorative excess in favor of dense, information-rich utility.

The design movement combines **Structural Modernism** with **High-Density Utility**:
- **Absolute Authority:** Grounded deep tones establish solemnity, institutional integrity, and constitutional gravitas.
- **Micro-Precision Engineering:** 1px structured grid lines, sharp typography hierarchy, precise visual containers, and monospace/tabular figures that respect complex legal citations (e.g., AIR, SCC, SCR references).
- **Dual-Register Reading:** Clean distinction between operational interface surfaces (system controls, metadata filters, telemetry) and sacred constitutional text (statutory provisions, judicial dicta, verbatim acts).
- **Clarity Under Pressure:** High-contrast signaling for compliance checkpoints, verification levels, and statutory overrides without aesthetic clutter.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around institutional gravitas, clarity, and legal certainty. Surfaces are built from cool slate tones, punctuated by functional signal pigments.

### Functional Roles & Accents
- **Primary (`#1E40AF` / `#1D4ED8`):** Royal Navy governs active legal research workflows, primary state transitions, active filter triggers, and authoritative links.
- **Institutional Anchor (`#0F172A` / `#1E293B`):** Deep Slate anchors global navigation sidebars, dossier headers, terminal-grade query engines, and high-impact structural divides.
- **Heritage Ochre (`#D97706` / `#B45309`):** Reserved for statutory seals, gazette notices, precedent authority rankings, and high-bench constitutional benchmarks.
- **Jurisprudential Emerald (`#059669` / `#10B981`):** Applied to verified case citations, affirmed precedents, Supreme Court cross-validations, and 100% confidence markers.
- **Sanction Crimson (`#DC2626`):** Dedicated to overturned judgments, non-maintainable claims, high-risk procedural lapses, and jurisdictional warnings.
- **Surface Scale:** Base background is `#F8FAFC`, elevated panel background is `#FFFFFF`, with structured demarcation surfaces rendered via `#F1F5F9` and hairline dividers in `#E2E8F0`.

## Typography

This design system uses a strict dual-font typographic hierarchy designed for enterprise legal research:

1. **System & Analytics Layer (Inter):** Neo-grotesque sans-serif utilized across all operational tools, argument trees, bench analytics, navigation, and tabular briefs. Tuned with tight letter tracking (`-0.02em` on titles) for dense data consumption. Numerical outputs enforce OpenType tabular figures (`tnum`, `cv05`) for citation IDs and timeline benchmarks.
2. **Statutory & Ratio Decidendi Layer (Merriweather):** Classical, authoritative serif reserved strictly for verbatim statutory excerpts (e.g., Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, Constitution of India), judicial rulings, court orders, and cross-referenced clauses. The serif typeface introduces intellectual pace, preventing optical fatigue during exhaustive brief reviews.
3. **Citation & Metadata Layer (JetBrains Mono):** Standardized for Section citations (e.g., `BNS § 103(1)`), Diary Numbers, Court Room designations, and AI confidence telemetry.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an enterprise-density 12-column layout designed to maximize visible intelligence per viewport while retaining high scanning velocity:

- **Desktop (1280px+):** Tri-pane architecture.
  - Left: Fixed 260px collapsible institutional navigation & case tree.
  - Center: Fluid workspace (min 680px) displaying active arguments, drafting canvas, and chat intelligence.
  - Right: Contextual 380px citation dossier panel with verified law reports, act references, and judicial benchmarks.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** Dual-pane layout. Navigation condenses to a 56px icon rail; contextual citation panel slides out as an overlay sheet on invocation.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked stream with fixed bottom utility bar for quick queries, voice dictation, and bench reference lookups.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Tight 4px baseline grid. Compact padding scales (`space-sm` at 8px, `space-md` at 12px) provide the compact density demanded by legal practitioners parsing hundreds of pages of case law.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on heavy drop shadows; it relies on **structural borders, hairline dividers, and subtle luminance layering**:

- **Borders over Shadows:** Surfaces are delineated by crisp 1px borders (`#E2E8F0` on light surfaces; `#334155` on dark frames).
- **Z-Index Layering (Tonal Stepping):**
  - `Level 0 (Canvas Base)`: Flat `#F8FAFC`.
  - `Level 1 (Panels & Legal Cards)`: Pure `#FFFFFF` resting within a 1px `#E2E8F0` border.
  - `Level 2 (Active Focus & Flyouts)`: Elevated using a razor-thin ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
  - `Level 3 (Modal Overlays / Citation Inspectors)`: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)` with a high-contrast `#0F172A` hairline border.
- **Active Interactive States:** Subtle micro-shift border change (`#CBD5E1` transitioning to `#1E40AF`) rather than card movement or float animations.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a **Soft Structural Geometry (Level 1)**:

- Standard controls, inputs, data badges, and citation markers adhere to a crisp `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius.
- Cards, modal containers, and elevated dossier modules use `0.5rem` (8px) (`rounded-lg`).
- High-level preview drawers or canvas frames cap at `0.75rem` (12px) (`rounded-xl`).
- Pure pill shapes (`rounded-full`) are strictly forbidden except for circular status pulses and legal bench verification dots, preserving an austere, architectural aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#1E40AF` fill, `#FFFFFF` Inter Medium typography, 4px border radius, 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05). Hover state: `#1D4ED8`. Focus: 2px offset ring in `#93C5FD`.
- **Institutional / Secondary:** Deep slate outline (`#E2E8F0` border, `#0F172A` text), `#FFFFFF` background. Hover state: `#F8FAFC` background with `#0F172A` border.
- **Tertiary / Utility:** Ghost style with no border, Inter 13px Medium text in `#475569`, transitioning to `#0F172A` on hover.

### Statutory Legal Cards & Precedent Cards
- Built on a `#FFFFFF` container with a 1px `#E2E8F0` border and 8px border radius.
- Divided into three structured horizontal zones:
  1. *Metadata Header:* Monospaced docket number, date of ruling, quorum (e.g., "3-Judge Bench"), and verified precedent badge.
  2. *Statutory Excerpt Area:* Subtle `#F8FAFC` inset background carrying verbatim legal text set in Merriweather with a left 3px authoritative border accent (Amber `#D97706` for ongoing review, Emerald `#059669` for settled ratio).
  3. *Collapsible Citation Footer:* Micro-accordion revealing cross-citations (SCC, AIR), citation reliability score, and paragraph bookmarking.

### Chips & Badges
- **Statutory Authority Tag:** Ochre tint (`#FEF3C7` background, `#92400E` text, 1px `#FDE68A` border), font size 11px uppercase bold.
- **Verified Benchmark Tag:** Emerald tint (`#ECFDF5` background, `#065F46` text, 1px `#A7F3D0` border), paired with a 6px green circular status indicator.
- **Jurisdiction Tag:** Slate tint (`#F1F5F9` background, `#334155` text, 1px `#CBD5E1` border).

### Input Fields & Legal Prompt Workspace
- Flat `#FFFFFF` background encased in a 1px `#CBD5E1` border.
- Inner padding: 8px vertical, 12px horizontal.
- Typography: Inter 14px regular with tabular figure support.
- Focused state: Border snaps to `#1E40AF` with a non-blur 1px outer ring in `#1E40AF`.
- Complex query inputs feature integrated keyboard shortcuts (e.g., `⌘ + ↵` to run brief analysis, `§` for quick section trigger).

### Dual-Mode Switches & Judicial Filter Controls
- Segmented pill switches featuring a 2px offset border in `#E2E8F0`.
- Active segment transitions cleanly using `#0F172A` background and `#FFFFFF` text, providing immediate structural feedback for switches like "Summary vs. Full Judgement" and "BNS vs. IPC Mapping".

### Warnings & Procedural Blocks
- Full-bleed bordered callouts featuring 4px left-border accents in `#DC2626` over a `#FEF2F2` background, accompanied by monospaced procedural citations outlining non-compliance or procedural bar parameters.
