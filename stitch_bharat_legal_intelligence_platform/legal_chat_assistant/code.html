Executive Legal Summary

The nocturnal detention at 11:00 PM without prompt intimation of substantive grounds constitutes a flagrant breach of the constitutional guarantee enshrined under Article 22(1) as well as the imperative procedural safeguards mandated by Section 50 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (BNSS). The denial of contemporaneous legal counsel renders the ongoing police custody prima facie unlawful and subject to constitutional habeas corpus jurisdiction.

Applicable Statutory Framework

menu_book Art. 21 — Protection of Life & Liberty balance Art. 22(1) — Right to be Informed & Counsel policy BNSS Sec. 50 — Mandatory Grounds Disclosure schedule BNSS Sec. 58 — 24hr Magistrate Remand Mandate

Procedural Action Pathway for Senior Counsel

1

Demand Physical Delivery of Arrest Memo (Form 2 under BNSS)

Dispatch immediate written notice to the Station House Officer (SHO) and jurisdictional ACP demanding the time-stamped arrest memo specifying precise grounds, inspecting officer ID, and designated relative notification record.

Immediate Habeas Corpus Writ Petition Before High Court (Art. 226) / SC (Art. 32)

Invoke emergency roster mentioning before the Hon'ble Chief Justice / Vacation Bench alleging unauthorized confinement violating Art. 21/22 and non-adherence to the mandatory arrest procedural guidelines.
